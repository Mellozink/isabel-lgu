About Isabel

A Municipality of Pride & Progress

Isabel is a 4th-class municipality in the province of Leyte, Philippines. Situated along the scenic shores of the Visayan Sea, Isabel is known for its rich agricultural lands, thriving local industries, and the warmth of its people.

Home to 24 barangays, the municipality has been steadily growing through good governance, infrastructure development, and a deep commitment to the welfare of every citizen. Isabel takes pride in its cultural heritage, natural resources, and community spirit.
